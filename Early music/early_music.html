Early Music Festival

Rebranding of Early Music Festival which takes place in Saint-Petersburg every year. It's an international festival presenting baroque, renaissance, medieval and classical music in it's authentic perfomance.

Year 2020
Study project

The logo consists of a type block and a diagonal line. The dynamic ascending line refers to the movements of the bow and baton of the conductor and symbolizes the aspiration to the high.

Identity concept.
The graphic elements of the identity are based on the diagonal line found in the festival logo. By transforming a simple stroke into linear patterns and using different thicknesses and directions of these lines, I tried to show the expressiveness and dynamics of the music.
